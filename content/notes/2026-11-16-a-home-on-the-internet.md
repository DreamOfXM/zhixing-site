最近看到了一个开发者的个人网站。吸引我的，倒不完全是网站做得多好，而是一件很简单的事：自己做过的东西，终于有了一个统一的去处。

我也想有这样一个地方。把做过的项目、开源的东西放在一起，也留一点位置给阅读，给平时偶尔冒出来的想法。

> 作品可以点开，书可以翻阅，想法可以留下。

有人来，可以试试一个小工具；也可以打开一本书，或者读一篇随笔。不需要多么宏大，希望它简单、舒服，又有一点自己的气息。

不必一下子填满。先把这个地方搭起来，再把值得留下的东西，一点一点放进来。

<<EN>>

I saw another developer's personal website recently. What stayed with me wasn't how impressive the site was, but something much simpler: everything they had made had a home.

That's what I'd like to have too. My projects and open-source work in one place, alongside the things I read and the occasional thought worth keeping.

> A project you can open. A book you can turn to. A thought you can return to.

A visitor could try a tool, open a book, or read a note. None of it needs to be grand. I'd rather it feel clear, comfortable, and alive.

I don't need to fill it all at once. A home on the internet can grow slowly, one piece at a time.
